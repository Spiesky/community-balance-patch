# What the community says is wrong with the balance, and what the rebalance does about it

Research on 2026-09-16 against the current game (the dump is patch 7.1 or later: Dread Maw 1100, Slaangors MD 26,
Cockatrice 1050). Two communities with different complaints: the multiplayer players keep a peer-reviewed list of
balance recommendations per patch (Total Tavern and Vermin League discords), and the campaign players complain
about what the AI cannot handle. The lists from 6.0 to 7.1 overlap enough to say what is *persistently* egregious.

## 3. What this says about the rebalance

**Where the two agree, it is the elite infantry.** Both the multiplayer list and CA's own patch notes say elite
infantry is not worth its price, and the ladder's biggest single theme is exactly that: guards to 80 at Chosen
quality, Chaos Warriors and Chosen up, the heavy dead up. That is the strongest external support the ladder has.

**Where they disagree, it is prices of monsters and beasts in the flat-priced castes.** The model prices a Great
Eagle at 1025 and the community wants it stronger *and* cheaper; the same for the Lammasu, the Great Unclean One,
the Feral Stegadon, the Brood Horror, the Beast of Nurgle, the bolt throwers, the Fell Bats. The pattern is one
finding: in those castes the price layer's "bargain" verdicts are mostly the model over-rating a unit whose
weakness is behaviour (accuracy, mobility, breath in melee, crumbling, being a slow target), not stats. The
safest reading is that **in the flat castes the price layer should only cut prices, never raise them**, and
that the lore entries there should keep vanilla prices.

**Three real lore-versus-meta conflicts** need a decision, not a model fix: Centigors (the study raises them, the
meta says they already dominate), Exalted Bloodletters (the ladder raises them, the campaign calls them the
strongest infantry in the game), and the Thundertusk (over-priced by the model, over-performing by the meta).

**Two things the model cannot see and should stop pretending to**: Wrathmongers' splash interaction and the
Sky Lantern's accuracy. Both belong on a keep list.

## 4. Amendments (applied 2026-09-16)

1. Price layer, flat castes: cut only, never raise (`rebalance_solve.py`, the price branch).
2. Lore entries in flat castes: the price follows the power along the caste's line, corrected for the residual up
   to the cap, but never ends above vanilla (a stronger Great Eagle stays at 750; a ×1.7 Bloodthirster that was
   over-priced ends near 2000, not cut on top of the raise).
3. Ladder: the Gor Herd raise excludes the dual-weapon variant; Centigors and the Thundertusk are `keep` pending
   a decision; Exalted Bloodletters ×1.15 instead of ×1.33; Sky Lantern, Brood Horror, Beast of Nurgle, the
   bats, the Eagle Claw and Reaper Bolt Throwers and Wrathmongers are `keep`.
4. Kossars −25, Streltsi −25, Akshina −50 (`price_add` entries: the community's numbers where the model is blind).
5. Chosen and Daemonettes: the ladder's quality raise at the vanilla price (`hold_price`).

Effect: price-only changes fell from 101 to 46 units; 479 units change and all are in the pack.
