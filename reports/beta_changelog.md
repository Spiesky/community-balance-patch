# Community Balance Patch beta: what's in the pack

43 units. The beta ships only two changes; everything else is a proposal (see `reports/changelog.md`) waiting for players to test and argue about.

**Lore elites are fewer and far stronger.** Blood Knights, Grail Knights, Grail Guardians and the Swords of Chaos. A unit made smaller never has less total health than vanilla, and its price never rises faster than its strength.

**Gunpowder hits hard and reloads slow.** Handguns, rifles, jezzails, blunderbusses and pistols fire a 60% heavier volley and take 50% longer to reload. Damage over time barely changes; how you use them does.
