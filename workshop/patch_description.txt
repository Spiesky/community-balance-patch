A campaign balance mod that stays close to vanilla. Units should be worth what they cost, the lore elites should actually feel elite, and guns should hit like guns. All the numbers are public on GitHub and anyone can argue with them.

[b]Alpha 0.2.1[/b], built for game version 9.0. Full list of changes, unit by unit: [url=https://github.com/Spiesky/community-balance-patch/blob/main/reports/beta_changelog.md]here[/url]. It's an early alpha and a work in progress, so some things will be off. Play it and tell me.

[h2]What's in it[/h2]

258 units change. Right now that's four things:

[list]
[*]Lore elites are fewer but way stronger. Every Blood Knight is a vampire now, so you get 24 instead of 60, each one hitting like two and a half of the old ones. Grail Knights (32) and Grail Guardians (24) work the same way, Swords of Chaos get a big buff, and Depth Guard get the Lahmian Handmaiden treatment: 32 drowned bodyguards instead of 60, each nearly twice as tough. A smaller unit never ends up with less total health than vanilla. The catch is fewer swings: they shred elites and big targets but clear chaff slower.
[*]Guns hit harder and reload slower. Handguns, rifles, jezzails, blunderbusses and pistols do 60% more per volley, take 50% longer to reload and carry less ammo (Handgunners get 14 volleys instead of 22). Total damage over a battle is about the same and so is the price. The difference is how you play them: get into position, fire, then pull back or reload somewhere safe.
[*]Elite infantry like Greatswords, Bestigors, Slayers, Ironbreakers and Exalted daemons (23 units in all) get up to about 17% more health and damage, same price.
[*]A bunch of changes players have been asking for, inspired by the Total Tavern and Vermin League balance list (patch 7.1+) and general player complaints. Chaos Warriors, Chosen, Temple Guard, Phoenix Guard, Black Orcs, Great Eagles, more responsive artillery and lots more, 195 units counting regiments of renown. I skipped what CA already did in 8.1 and a few buffs to units that are already too strong in campaign.
[/list]

This mod only changes regular units. Lords, heroes, spells, auto-resolve and other campaign stuff will be a separate mod later. I've measured every unit in the game against its price and lore, but the rest of that stays a proposal on GitHub until people have tested it.

[h2]Things you'll notice[/h2]

[list]
[*]Lore elites cost more to recruit and keep. Blood Knights are about 30% more for 24 than they were for 60, Swords of Chaos go from 1500 to 2400. Grail Knights and Grail Guardians cost about the same as before.
[*]Gunners run out of ammo sooner and stand around reloading longer. That's intended, but tell me if it isn't fun.
[*]Some of the community changes were made with multiplayer in mind and come with nerfs: Zombies and Skeleton Horse Archers +25 gold, Ruglud's Armoured Orcs +50, Wrathmongers -4 melee defence and -5 leadership, Gor Herds -1 damage. If any of those feel wrong in campaign, let me know.
[/list]

[h2]Campaign first[/h2]

It's balanced for campaign, and I test it in custom battles. Recruitment cost and upkeep follow the multiplayer price. For multiplayer, both players need it.

[h2]Want to help?[/h2]

[list]
[*]Just play it and tell me in the comments what felt off.
[*]Run a quick test, about 20 minutes. The one I need most is [url=https://github.com/Spiesky/community-balance-patch/blob/main/units/blood_knights.md]Blood Knights vs Demigryph Knights[/url]. Post what happened in the discussions.
[*]Use the optional [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810476307]Battle Logger[/url]. It saves how every unit did to a text file. Drop the file on [url=https://spiesky.github.io/community-balance-patch/]this page[/url] to see what's in it, then paste the summary here or send the full log to GitHub.
[*]Got a change in mind? Post it with numbers, here or [url=https://github.com/Spiesky/community-balance-patch]on GitHub[/url].
[/list]

How changes get decided is [url=https://github.com/Spiesky/community-balance-patch/blob/main/docs/HOW_DECISIONS_ARE_MADE.md]written up here[/url]. While the mod is small I make the calls. From the next release, every change gets posted with its reason a week before it ships, and a test result can reopen anything, even stuff that's already in.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*]Data only, no scripts.
[*]It changes unit stats, prices, missile weapons and how some artillery and mounts move, so it'll clash with other mods that edit the same units.
[*]Works alongside the Community Bug Fix Mod: their Slayer Pirates fix wins and those units stay vanilla. Haven't tested the two together in game yet.
[*]Back up your save before adding or removing it mid-campaign.
[*]After a game update I rebuild it as soon as I can. The game version it was built for is at the top.
[/list]
