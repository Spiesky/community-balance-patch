Title: This week's test: Blood Knights (Lances) against Demigryph Knights

The patch turns 60 Blood Knights into 24, each with two and a half times the health and the blows. My two combat models disagree on whether 24 of them beat Demigryph Knights. One custom battle settles more than both models.

The recipe (about twenty minutes):

1. Custom battle, unit size Ultra, battle difficulty Normal, time limit 60 Minutes, map Crossroads - Multiplayer or any flat, open map (say which).
2. You set up both armies. Yours (Vampire Counts): a Vampire Lord on foot, no spells, and one unit of Blood Knights (Lances). The AI's (The Empire): a General of the Empire on foot and one unit of Demigryph Knights (the plain ones, not Halberds).
3. Order your lord to attack the enemy lord. Charge the Demigryph Knights head on with the Blood Knights and start a stopwatch. No further orders.
4. When one of the two cavalry units is dead or routs for good: pause. Write down who won, the winner's health bar as a share, its models left, and the time. Then quit the battle.
5. Three runs. If you have time, swap sides and do three more, and run it once with the patch switched off.
