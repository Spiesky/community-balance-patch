Blood Knights are the first unit under review. The page with the test wanted, the idea and the numbers is here: [units/blood_knights.md](https://github.com/Spiesky/community-balance-patch/blob/main/units/blood_knights.md)

Short version: 60 riders become **24 vampires**, each with two and a half times the health and the blows, and a charge to match. The price goes from 1550 to 2025 (sword and shield) and from 1650 to 2150 (lances) in beta 0.2.0; in the first beta it was 2475 and 2250. The two combat models I have disagree on whether 24 of them beat Demigryph Knights, so I need real battles.

The test: [docs/TESTING.md](https://github.com/Spiesky/community-balance-patch/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md), about twenty minutes. Post what you see here, on the Workshop page, or with the **Test report** form.
