# Blood Knights

**Status:** open for feedback. Both variants (sword and shield, lances).

## Why they're disputed

The current proposal is the biggest single change in the patch, and it hasn't been tested in game yet.

## Lore

Every rider of the Blood Dragon order is a vampire (tabletop around WS5 S5 T5 W2 A2), frenzied, in plate, on a nightmare steed. The game gives each one the health of a mortal knight and fields a full-size regiment of them.

## Current proposal

Fewer, much stronger riders, each worth roughly what a vampire should be:

| | models | melee attack / defence | HP per model (bonus) | weapon damage | price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood Knights (sword and shield), vanilla | 60 | 46 / 54 | 120 | 38 + 21 vs infantry | 1550 |
| proposed | **24** | 48 / 56 | 155 | 50 + 27 vs infantry | **1650** |
| Blood Knights (lances), vanilla | 60 | 42 / 42 | 120 | 35 + 19 vs large | 1650 |
| proposed | **24** | 47 / 47 | 217 | 64 + 34 vs large | **1750** |

Model counts are as the database stores them. HP is the per-model bonus, not the total.

## What we don't know yet

Our two models disagree. The frontage model says 24 vampires hold their own because the enemy can't all reach them at once. The second-by-second simulator says 24 riders get outnumbered: at equal gold, Demigryph Knights beat vanilla Blood Knights with 38% left and the proposed ones with 76% left. **Only in-game battles can settle it.**

## Open questions for you

1. Should Blood Knights be a small elite unit (fewer, far stronger vampires), or a full regiment that's only moderately stronger?
2. Campaign vs multiplayer: are they too strong, too weak or about right in each right now?
3. Should their price go up with the power, or stay near vanilla?

## Tests we need

- Proposed Blood Knights against Demigryph Knights, Grail Knights and Chaos Knights at equal gold.
- Proposed Blood Knights charging into Halberdiers and into Chaos Warriors.
- The same fights with vanilla Blood Knights, as the baseline.
